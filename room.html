One day, several people.

A room is one campaign that several people walk around at once. The day's calls are split between you: whoever opens a call answers it, and the verdict goes to everybody.

The five characters somebody read out.

Everyone in a room shares one campaign, one clock and one set of calls — it is not your own save, and finishing a day in a room does not touch the game you are playing alone. A call being answered by one person is the rule that keeps six browsers from writing over each other, so if a teammate has opened something, you will be told whose it is rather than shown a second copy of it.
