Reasoning Games for Naval-Relevant Science

Four browser-based games, each teaching a habit of mind the Navy runs on — and each teaching it by making the player use real science.

Integrate incomplete evidence. Estimate what you cannot measure directly. Put cause before effect. And resist the loud-but-wrong answer. The topics below are drawn from the full catalogs and mapped to the technical areas the Office of Naval Research supports — from undersea and oceanography to autonomy, cyber, power, materials, and warfighter performance. All four are self-contained, offline, and built for STEM learning.

The unifying claim: across all four games, the transferable skill is calibrated reasoning under uncertainty — integrate evidence, estimate what you can't measure, order cause and effect, and resist the sensational conclusion. That is the reasoning behind training, analysis, autonomy assurance, and STEM workforce development. Each game is browser-based, self-contained, and teaches its science by making the player use it. Topics shown are a representative selection; the full catalogs span natural hazards, structures, energy, medicine, computing, law, history, and culture.
