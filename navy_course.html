Reasoning for Naval Science

A 15-week course that teaches naval science by playing it: each week turns one real concept — how sonar hears a contact, how a radar builds a track, how a ship fights flooding or fire — into a puzzle you can only solve by understanding the system, not memorizing facts. Work straight through or jump around; tap a week to play. A week's circle fills only when you actually finish and win it — there's no marking it done by hand. Every odd lesson you complete unlocks a new ship for the Battleship matches after weeks 5, 10, and 15.
