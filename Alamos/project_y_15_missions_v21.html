Project Y

Build a research organization capable of finishing an unprecedented wartime physics and engineering program. You do not solve the project by clicking a single correct answer. You assign leaders, decide which work receives scarce funding, read the historical record, and spend your own time where it can accelerate the laboratory.

Educational scope: this game models leadership, nuclear-physics reasoning, experimental evidence, and project organization. It deliberately omits weapon dimensions, material quantities, manufacturing recipes, assembly procedures, and other operational design details.
