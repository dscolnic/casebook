ReadingWhere this campaign stands

The worst curriculum delivery in the set: 2 of 10 equations computed. Four of the eight gaps sit on instruments and may be Phase 0 phantoms, but four do not, and two equations — a building’s natural period and base shear — are mentioned nowhere, with base shear also listed as an absent concept. Twenty-two CHOICE stops, seven select-only concepts, and zero OPERATE stops — the only game in the set with none. The place, by contrast, is one of the strongest: granite bench above, liquefied fill below, and a 1.8 m fault scarp you walk between them.

FunWhat this subject and this place can carry

TALLY

strongstop 19 or 20 · HAZ / SEIS

Aftershocks accumulate and the rate falls as a power law. Omori’s law is mentioned at fifteen stops and computed at none, and "a rate is not a schedule" is currently a CHOICE. TALLY is the format whose subject is precisely that an early number moves because there is not enough behind it.

serves
n(t) = K/(c+t)^p — the most-mentioned uncomputed equation in the repo
needs
an early batch that flatters the rate, and a pass mark that stays unprinted

PROBE

strongstop 26 or 40 · GEO

Walk from the granite bench across the scarp onto the fill, taking a reading at each station, and name where the ground changes. Site amplification is mentioned at thirteen stops and computed at none, and the place was built to be walked exactly this way.

serves
A_soft / A_rock — and the scarp stops being scenery
needs
stations along the real traverse; one honest break that is not the worst number

SWEEP

strongstop 1 or 11 · SEIS / STRUCT

Building period against ground period, and the peak is where they meet. Concept 8 — natural period, and resonance between building and ground — is select-only on one stop, and it is the single most consequential idea in earthquake engineering.

serves
concept 8, and T ≈ 0.1 N, which is mentioned nowhere at all
needs
a findable peak, unmarked

SPOT

strongstop 8 or 9 · SAFE / HAZ

A placarding criterion replaced mid-round with nothing announcing it. "Green does not mean safe" is already the game’s own sentence; a rapid-assessment crew whose criterion is revised on day three is what actually happened in every event this game is drawn from.

serves
concept 22 — rapid assessment against detailed evaluation, currently select-only
risk
score only the discriminating items

TRIAL

strongthe bay

An assessment round driven across the scarp: which buildings first, and the walk between them is the geology. Order is the answer — the school before the parade building, because four hundred children arrive on Monday.

serves
concept 22 and triage order
risk
world work, and the most expensive item in this plan

HOLD

mediumSTRUCT · shoring

Hold a shoring jack’s load inside a band while the structure settles and aftershocks arrive. Real, and the physics is statics rather than the dynamics the syllabus is short of.

BELT

defer—

Sorting placards at speed would teach the criterion and reward speed on a subject where speed killed people. Defer on tone.

The order of work

1. Phase 0 — teach the coverage check to read an instrument’s own board, and re-baseline curriculum-debt.json. Rewriting a stop that already teaches the thing is the exact failure the check exists to prevent. 2. Snapshot. 3. Edit the book, never the generated content — tools/import-book.mjs books/aftershock.yml aftershock --verify. 4. npm run traps: every instrument’s trap must fire. 5. npm run check aftershock and npm run drive aftershock, right answer and wrong — ask what a player who understood nothing would score before believing any pass mark. 6. --against the snapshot. A changed format is reported; a changed objective fails.
