THE EXPANDING UNIVERSE (theme: qd_hubble) — PLACES AND LOCATIONS
Observer · Mountain Observatory
Course: Astronomy — the expanding universe. Grade 9, nine stops, three levels
Grade 9. One mission is called a Level; a stop is "a room".

A QUICK DISCOVERY: nine stops on a 3 x 3 spine, played in one sitting. No
warm-up runs and no second-day model — WARMUP_MIN_DAYS is four, and a schedule
written for a fortnight of mornings would open two of three cards on a run the
player did not come for. Every card teaches its method before it asks
anything.

Run it:  cd gamekit && THEME=qd_hubble npm run dev

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS PLACE IS THIS PLACE  (from the source file that builds it)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
plan.js — the mountain observatory's working floor, as data.

One corridor, four rooms, and the layout is the argument: the two axes of the
plot are measured on opposite sides of it, by different people, on different
nights, and the room where they finally meet is at the far end.

Units are metres. +z runs along the corridor, +x is to the right of it.

The plate vault is the deepest room on the plan because years of glass in
numbered drawers is what it holds, and the diagram room is the widest because
its long wall is one plot.
