Analyst · High-Redshift Supernova Team
Course: Astronomy and cosmology — the accelerating universe. Grade 9, nine
stops, three levels
Grade 9. One mission is called a Level; a stop is "a place".

A QUICK DISCOVERY: nine stops on a 3 x 3 spine, played in one sitting. No
warm-up runs and no second-day model — WARMUP_MIN_DAYS is four, and a schedule
written for a fortnight of mornings would open two of three cards on a run the
player did not come for. Every card teaches its method before it asks
anything.

Run it:  cd gamekit && THEME=qd_accel npm run dev

WHY THIS PLACE IS THIS PLACE  (from the source file that builds it)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
site.js — Cerro Vela Survey Operations, as data.

A Quick Discovery reskin of themes/bring_them_home/site.js. The geometry is
that file's, unchanged, because themes/qd_accel/props.js and world.js are
copies of that game's and are placed off these numbers. What is changed is
everything a player reads: the name, the four areas of study, the console
labels and the three plot boards.

The place is the analysis floor of a supernova survey: a single large volume
with the floor stepped down toward a wall of plot boards, teams sitting as
rows rather than in rooms, a ring corridor behind it and two rooms off that.

Looking at the floor from the back doors:

SPEC and PRESS are the two rooms off the ring corridor behind that, one each
side. Each row sits one step higher than the row in front of it, so the whole
floor looks down at the boards.

1. THE AREAS — 4 rooms carrying a group
==============================================================================

PHOT — Photometry
  Photometry Desk   [PHOT]  side w, z -11.6 … -8
  Light curves, magnitudes and the residual plot everything ends up on.
  Calls: Fit the light curve · Standardise it · Put it on the diagram · Ready
  to hand over

COSMO — Cosmology Console
  Cosmology Console   [COSMO]  side spine, z 9.4 … 13
  Expansion histories, laid over the same points and read off the leftovers.
  Calls: State the model · Fit the sample · Read the residuals · Ready to hand
  over

SPEC — Spectroscopy & Calibration
  Spectroscopy Room   [SPEC]  side w, z 20 … 40
  What the lines say the redshift is, and whether dust is in the way.
  Calls: Take the arc · Measure the line · Check the colour · Ready to hand
  over

PRESS — Publication Room
  Publication Room   [PRESS]  side e, z 20 … 40
  What the result is allowed to say out loud, in the order it is allowed to
  say it.
  Calls: Draft the claim · Separate the mechanism · Get it argued with · Ready
  to hand over
