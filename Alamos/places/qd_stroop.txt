STROOP EFFECT (theme: qd_stroop) — PLACES AND LOCATIONS
Research Assistant · Reaction-Time Laboratory
Course: Cognitive psychology — the Stroop effect, 1935. Grade 9, nine stops,
three levels
Grade 9. One mission is called a Level; a stop is "a room".

A QUICK DISCOVERY: nine stops on a 3 x 3 spine, played in one sitting. No
warm-up runs and no second-day model — WARMUP_MIN_DAYS is four, and a schedule
written for a fortnight of mornings would open two of three cards on a run the
player did not come for. Every card teaches its method before it asks
anything.

Run it:  cd gamekit && THEME=qd_stroop npm run dev

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS PLACE IS THIS PLACE  (from the source file that builds it)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
plan.js — the reaction-time laboratory, as data.

One corridor, four working rooms off it, and the rooms nobody works in that
make it a building rather than a diagram. The place has a shape the campaign
uses: the booth and the tabulation room are down one side, the chronoscope
bench and the distribution wall down the other, so a time measured in one room
crosses the corridor twice before it becomes a difference anybody argues about.

Units are metres. +z runs along the corridor, +x is to the right of it.

The distribution wall is the deepest room on the plan. Eight hundred times
plotted as a distribution needs wall, and a histogram in a room the size of an
office is a graph rather than a wall of data — which is the difference the
third level is about.

`ceiling: false`. A 1935 university building has a plastered ceiling with
pendant fittings under it and no tile grid, and the grid is the loudest
anachronism available in a room like this.

  Tabulation Room   [TAB]  side w, z 25 … 37
  Sheets, a middle value rather than an average, and an error column nobody is

  Calls: Check the sheets · Take the medians · Enter both columns · Ready to
  hand over

CHRON — Chronoscope Bench
  Chronoscope Bench   [CHRON]  side e, z 5 … 17
  An instrument that starts on a card and stops on a voice, with a delay at
  both ends of it.
  Calls: Calibrate · Time the block · Take the difference · Ready to hand over

WALL — Distribution Wall
  Distribution Wall   [WALL]  side e, z 19 … 33
  Eight hundred times pinned up as two piles, and four accounts of the effect
  that all fit them.
  Calls: Pin the times · Read the shape · Say what it does not show · Ready to
  hand over
