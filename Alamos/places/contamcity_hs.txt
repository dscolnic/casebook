WHY THIS PLACE IS THIS PLACE  (from the source file that builds it)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is an EDITION. The place is themes/contamcity/site.js — same world,
same cast, same grade, a different course. What follows is that file.

site.js — Riverton, as data.

The design book names forty-five locations but never says what kind of place
this is. It is a working river city: a freight yard on the high side, the
drinking-water intake and the laboratories along a cross street, and the
river itself across the north edge, behind a bank the player cannot walk off.

Layout, looking down (-Z is north, toward the river):

       ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~ RIVER ~~~~~~~~~~~~~~          z = -112
       ============ river bank ==========           z = -66
       ------------ riverfront road -----           z = -52

    [ Water Intake ]                [ Air & Plume ]  z = -34

                 [ Freight Yard ]                    z =  92

Two things here are load-bearing rather than decorative:

  · The spawn point at (0, 36) sits on the avenue with nothing within ten
    metres. A prop dropped over the spawn welds the player in place — the
    scene renders perfectly and W does nothing.
  · Every building's footprint becomes a graded pad inside groundHeight().
    outdoorTown registers them before the first height query, so the visible
    ground and the height function cannot disagree.

1. THE AREAS — 6 of them, one group each
==============================================================================

ENERGY — Reactions & Energy
  Reaction & Thermal Hall
  (-42, 76)   58 m from spawn   26 x 16 x 8.4
  Stoichiometry, calorimetry and kinetics — how much can react, how much heat,
  and how fast.
  Calls: Balance the reaction and find what limits it · Separate stored heat
  from ongoing reaction · Connect rate and activation energy to thermal
  feedback · State the condition under which the vessel is safe

TREAT — Treatment & Infrastructure
  Pilot Treatment Plant
  (44, 76)   59 m from spawn   28 x 18 x 8.8
  Corrosion, treatment trains, byproducts and the long tail — fixing it
  without making it worse.
  Calls: Identify the redox couples attacking the pipeline · Compare
  treatments by their complete consequences · Account for what the treatment
  moved rather than destroyed · Close the campaign with a monitoring and
  stewardship plan

IDENT — Molecular Identification
  Molecular Identification Lab
  (-54, 4)   63 m from spawn   24 x 15 x 7.4
  DELIVERY ROOM — the board for The Riverton Evidence Package is here.
  Composition, charge, separation and spectra — what the material in the
  containers actually is.
  Calls: Build the provisional identity list · Separate the mixture before
  naming it · Confirm identity with independent methods · Release a
  confidence-ranked identity dossier

QUANT — Quantitative Analysis
  Quantitative Analysis & QA
  (54, 4)   63 m from spawn   22 x 14 x 7
  Dilution, calibration, detection limits and uncertainty — turning a signal
  into a defensible number.
  Calls: Turn instrument signal into concentration · Put a calibration and a
  blank behind every number · Attach an uncertainty to the decision, not just
  the sample · Establish the release rule before the result arrives

GASES — Atmosphere & Gases
  Air & Plume Station
  (46, -34)   84 m from spawn   20 x 13 x 6.4
  Gas laws, plume behaviour, photochemistry — how the release moves and
  changes in air.
  Calls: Bound the release with a gas-law estimate · Separate transport from
  chemistry in the plume · Track the secondary products after the source stops
  · Issue a defensible exposure picture over time

WATER — Solutions & Water Chemistry
  Water Intake Laboratory
  (-48, -34)   85 m from spawn   22 x 14 x 7
  Polarity, partitioning, pH and equilibrium — where the contaminant goes once
  it is wet.
  Calls: Predict partitioning from molecular interactions · Bring the intake
  pH under control without masking risk · Show whether equilibrium moved the
  hazard or removed it · Account for the contaminant across water, sediment
  and solids
