# The warm-up runs: thirteen lines the cast replacement does not settle

The "Warm-up run cast replacement" section did what it was for and stopped one
line short of being executable. It says who may appear:

> All six warm-up run variants must use only this shipped cast: Nkemdi Okafor at
> Waterworks, Iona Vale at the Common, Rafi Noor at the Reef, Mei Chen at the
> Tip, Lena Costa at the School, Elias Shaw at the Turbine Yard, and Ada Pell at
> the Chapel.

**It does not say which of them belongs in which run.** One of the fourteen
legacy names resolved on its own, because its run happens to name a place from
that list: Carrying's `follow` run says "counts stock on the common", so Dan Pike
became Iona Vale, his title became her own, and the pronouns went with her —
that run tells the player to walk beside somebody for a paragraph, and it read
"he does not stop walking" about a woman.

The other thirteen name a job and no place, so nothing on this side can choose
without inventing. It was tried: matching the legacy job title against the
roster's roles produced Carrying's water engineer as the Island Resources
Officer, and Changeover's market trader as the price statistics lead. A name in
the wrong mouth reads as true, which is worse than a name nobody recognises.

## What to write

For each line below, name the shipped person. One name each is enough.

### Carrying Capacity

| run | the line as it stands |
| --- | --- |
| `greet` | "Calloway, the harbourmaster, wants you known to as many of them as you can get round" |
| `hunt` | "Mairead Sorley, the water engineer, has narrowed it to the seven standpipes" |
| `canvass` | "Dan Pike, who administers the grazing rights, will not put words in their mouths" |
| `evade` | "the abstraction figures before Dunmore, the council clerk, has checked them" |
| `tag` | "Petra Rask, the fisheries observer, leaves on the Friday sailing" |

`canvass` is about the graziers on the common and is almost certainly Iona Vale
again, but the run never says "common", so it is left for you rather than
guessed. The `trial-far` run also names a Blessing Okafor who runs the tip — a
different person from Nkemdi Okafor, and the note that "`Nkemdi` means Nkemdi
Okafor, not a separate person" does not cover her.

### Changeover

Its own section lists the eight shipped names and **no places at all**, so none
of its seven lines can resolve:

| run | the line as it stands |
| --- | --- |
| `greet` | "Ngozi Okonjo, the counter supervisor, wants you known to as many of the people on these four floors" |
| `trial-near` | "Bram Tulloch, who manages the note room, walks every new officer the length of the floor" |
| `follow` | "Kata Novotny, a market trader from Vend Street, brings her price cards up to the price room" |
| `hunt` | "Femi Adeyinka, the vault officer, has eight regional ledgers out across these four floors" |
| `canvass` | "Ngozi Okonjo, the counter supervisor, has forty people who have been on the plaza telephones" |
| `evade` | "before the chief statistician, Emil Radic, has certified the basket it rests on" |
| `tag` | "Levon Sarkis, the trade and reserves officer, closes the wire room at four" |

**`follow` needs a decision rather than a name.** Kata Novotny is deliberately
not staff — she is a market trader from Vend Street, and the run works because
the basket can be checked against something outside the building. Putting a
board member in her place removes the point of the run. Either she joins the
roster, or that run needs a different subject.

## Two things that come free with the name

The **job title** beside it is replaced with that person's own role from the
character bible, so nothing has to be written twice. The **pronouns** in the run
are corrected to theirs, which the character bible states. Both happen
automatically once the run has a name.
