v1.2 builds, imports and plays. Round 1's two blocking items are done: **The

## 1. Fifteen mission cards never say what the player decides

Unchanged, and it is fifteen of fifteen. Every stake in this set is written so
the player can see their own job in it:

> **Today you decide** whether to replace the failing seedling feed.

Yours say what is happening and stop. Your objective line already carries the
decision, so this is moving one clause up into the stake. Boomtown and Whiteout
both cleared this in a single pass.

## 2. Thirteen segues do not turn

The segue carries one mission into the next and its job is a **complication** —
*but*, *yet*, *so*, *now*, *that leaves*. Thirteen of fifteen state a fact and
stop; two more name no number, clock or person.

## 3. The opening card never says what the player is

> the opening never says what the player is

Your §1 calls the player "the junior biologist who connects clinic, genetics,
nursery and field evidence" and the card never does. One clause: *You are the
junior biologist on Pellow Head.*

## 4. Eleven equations are printed and never computed

The "Worth knowing first" box shows the day's equations, and on eleven of fifteen
days no stop that day computes the one shown:

```
day 1  shows  deficit = requirement − supply    and no stop that day uses it
day 2  shows  SA/V = 6/L for a cube             and no stop that day uses it
```

Either the day needs a stop that computes it, or the equation should come off
that day's card. On a biology course this matters more than it sounds: the SA/V
relation on day 2 is the mechanism of the whole mission and the player never
touches the numbers.

## 5. Ten terms are used and never introduced

`id`, `catalyst`, `NADPH`, `oxidized`, `II`, and the codons `GCU`, `GCC`, `GUU`,
`UGG`, `UGA`.

The codons are the interesting ones: a stop that asks a player to read a codon
table has to say what a codon is *on that card*, once. `NADPH` and
`photosystem II` are the same — name the term and gloss it on the spot, which is
the house rule for this whole catalogue: never delete the official word, put its
meaning beside it.

## 6. Six concept-ordering problems, and five passages with no questions

Six stops claim a concept whose stated prerequisite is not taught before it —
day 1 claims *"Macromolecules and hydrolysis"*, built on *"Water and carbon
chemistry"*, on the same day. Down from thirteen in v1.1.

And the five roster bios carry no question.

## 7. One PROBE station names its own treatment

M1 stop 3: the nitrogen-restored tray reads *"stored feed plus missing
nitrogen"*. It is not the answer, so the build no longer refuses it — but "plus
the nitrogen top-up" says the same thing without naming the deficit in a row the
player is reading for numbers.

beds under hoop tunnels, a marsh with reed beds and a boardwalk out to a hide
with birds circling, a grazing enclosure, a seed orchard, rock pools and a

sea cable stops being one"*, and is now the shore sampling point with a quadrat
frame against it and a marked stake at the tide line; the drum of armoured cable
in the store yard is a hose reel; the Ship's Store is the Shipping Store, staged
for the ship on the fifteenth.
