# planetary_defense_v2: put a syllabus number on every stop

Every stop in this campaign names a concept in its own words. The game needs
the course's own entry instead, because the sequencing checks grade *when* each
idea is first taught and they can only do that against one fixed list. Your
words are not being replaced — the tag stays in the bible; this adds the
number beside it.

The course is **Astronomy elective, with the mechanics of AP Physics 1**.

## The spine — the only 30 answers there are

 1. Celestial coordinates and angular measure
 2. Apparent and absolute magnitude; inverse-square brightness
 3. Albedo and the size–brightness degeneracy
 4. Light curves and rotation
 5. Telescopes: aperture, resolution, signal-to-noise
 6. Detectors and image artifacts
 7. Kepler's laws
 8. Orbital elements and orbit determination
 9. Gravitation, orbital speed and energy
10. Radar ranging, light travel time and Doppler
11. Uncertainty, error bars and covariance
12. Time and coordinate standards
13. Kinetic energy and impact energy scaling
14. Atmospheric entry: drag, ablation, airburst
15. Momentum transfer and deflection
16. Probability, risk and expected value
17. Scientific notation and unit conversion
18. Independent confirmation and data validation
19. Solar system architecture and near-Earth objects
20. Observing constraints: elongation, visibility windows, scheduling
21. Angular size and the small-angle formula
22. Parallax, baselines and distance
23. Thermal emission and infrared temperature
24. Phase angle, scattering and observing geometry
25. Impact velocity and gravitational focusing
26. Momentum conservation in collisions
27. Planetary perturbations and resonances
28. Monte Carlo methods and ensembles
29. Detection thresholds, false alarms and completeness
30. Crater and blast scaling; ground effects
