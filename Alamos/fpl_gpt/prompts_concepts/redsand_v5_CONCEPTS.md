# redsand_v5: put a syllabus number on every stop

Every stop in this campaign names a concept in its own words. The game needs
the course's own entry instead, because the sequencing checks grade *when* each
idea is first taught and they can only do that against one fixed list. Your
words are not being replaced — the tag stays in the bible; this adds the
number beside it.

The course is **AP Chemistry, the whole course, taught inside one propellant plant against a launch window**.

## The spine — the only 15 answers there are

 1. Atoms, molecules and ions, and the mole as a way of counting them
 2. Balanced equations and stoichiometry as an atom ledger
 3. The limiting reactant, theoretical yield and what is left over
 4. Gas behaviour and partial pressure
 5. Lewis structures, VSEPR, and what shape does to a molecule's behaviour
 6. Concentration, molarity and Beer-Lambert, and evidence that only looks independent
 7. Diagnosis from one mechanism, and whether measurement error could change it
 8. Heat, q = mcΔT, heating curves and an energy ledger
 9. Rate laws from experiment, the rate constant, and the controlled experiment
10. Catalysis, mechanisms, the rate-determining step and poisoning
11. Overfitting, the holdout test, residual structure and stress testing
12. Equilibrium: Kc, the ICE table, Q against K, and Le Châtelier
13. Coupled reactions, acid-base stoichiometry, and a whole-system balance
14. Redox and electrolysis: half-reactions, Q = It and Faraday's law
15. Composition against a specification, and thresholds chosen before the result

## The stops

Each row gives the stop, the tag the bible currently uses, and the entries whose
own keywords that tag comes closest to — **ranked, not chosen**. Where the
ranking is right, take it; where it is wrong, the number you write wins. A stop
may share a concept with another stop; a concept may go unused.
