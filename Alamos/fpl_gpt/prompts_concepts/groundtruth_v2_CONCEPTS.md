# groundtruth_v2: put a syllabus number on every stop

Every stop in this campaign names a concept in its own words. The game needs
the course's own entry instead, because the sequencing checks grade *when* each
idea is first taught and they can only do that against one fixed list. Your
words are not being replaced — the tag stays in the bible; this adds the
number beside it.

The course is **AP Physics C: Electricity & Magnetism, all five units, derived rather than quoted — every formula the course hands a student is produced here from the law it comes out of, on a lightning research station where each one is a measurement somebody has to take**.

 9. Equipotentials, and what they mean on the ground
10. Capacitance as a ratio, before it is a formula
11. Parallel plates: capacitance from the field between them
12. Dielectrics, dielectric strength and breakdown
13. Energy stored in a capacitor, and where the half comes from
14. Capacitors in series and parallel
15. Current as a flow, and current density
16. Resistance, resistivity and the ground as a resistor
17. Kirchhoff's rules on a real network
18. The RC circuit, and what a time constant means
19. Instrument bandwidth: what a slow instrument cannot see
20. Magnetic field of a current, and Ampère's law
21. Biot–Savart, and when the integral is worth doing
22. Force on a current in a field, and forces between conductors

28. The LR transient, and inductive volts against resistive volts
29. Energy in the magnetic field, and where a strike puts it
30. Measurement in the right regime: shunts, dividers and what they load
