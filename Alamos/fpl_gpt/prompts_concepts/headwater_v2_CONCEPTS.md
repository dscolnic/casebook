# headwater_v2: put a syllabus number on every stop

Every stop in this campaign names a concept in its own words. The game needs
the course's own entry instead, because the sequencing checks grade *when* each
idea is first taught and they can only do that against one fixed list. Your
words are not being replaced — the tag stays in the bible; this adds the
number beside it.

The course is **AP Calculus AB, and the first term of a university calculus sequence**.

## The spine — the only 28 answers there are

 1. Limits, one-sided limits, and where a limit fails to exist
 2. Continuity, and what a jump costs you
 3. The derivative as the limit of a difference quotient
 4. The power rule
 5. The product rule, and two factors moving at once
 6. The chain rule and composite quantities
 7. Implicit differentiation of a relation that will not rearrange
 8. Related rates
 9. Linear approximation, differentials, and the error the curvature costs
10. Indeterminate forms and L'Hopital's rule
11. Critical points, the first derivative test and optimisation
12. Concavity, the second derivative and points of inflection
13. Extreme values on a closed interval, including at the endpoints
14. Riemann sums, left and right endpoints, and the trapezoid rule
15. The definite integral as an accumulation, and its units
16. The fundamental theorem, both parts
17. Antiderivatives, and the constant an integration cannot recover
18. Substitution, and carrying the limits across with the variable
19. Average value of a function, and the mean value theorem
20. Separable differential equations
21. Exponential growth and decay, and half-life
22. Area between two curves, and the sign of the integrand
23. Volume by cross-sections
24. Units and dimensional bookkeeping through a derivative or an integral
25. Fitting a constant, and scoring it on data the fit never saw
26. Which input carries the uncertainty in a computed quantity
27. Predicting, then measuring what you predicted
28. Reading a quantity off the graph of its own derivative
