# Mission-opening accessibility review

Reviewed and rewrote all 120 mission-opening bodies in the eight latest round-13 bibles. Each now introduces the actual situation and ends with the player's decision. Replaced compressed workplace terms and lists of course procedures with concrete descriptions. The primer sections still supply the technical instruction.

Every revised body is shorter than its previous version in both words and characters. Word totals below use whitespace-separated words, consistently before and after.

## What changed

- Headwater: explained water behind the dam, opening gates, water reaching towns, and the decisions about warnings and storage. Removed unexplained terms such as head, drawdown, and release mix from the opening bodies. Day 1's body fell from 65 to 47 words; its title is now “Did the Water Really Rise?” and its navigation describes the desk showing reservoir water levels.
- The Trial: replaced procedure lists such as unions, conditional rates, blocking, and t procedures with the patient problem those procedures will investigate.
- Changeover: replaced compressed policy language with descriptions of jobs, household prices, borrowing, bank payments, and government support.
- Carrying Capacity: made groundwater, catch limits, pollution routes, dependable power, and arriving species concrete instead of assuming familiarity with phrases such as recharge ceiling and firm power.
- Planetary Defense: explained pictures, radar echoes, possible paths, and protective decisions without depending on phrases such as response envelope or missing physics.
- Ground Truth: connected the electrical measurements to actual wires, damage, instruments, and decisions about running another test.
- Safety Factor: described the ride movement, braking, loading, water supply, and rider protection before asking for an operating decision.
- Mars: described fuel supply, gas mixtures, water recycling, heat, and shared power in terms of getting the crew home.

## Checks completed

1. Read all 120 original opening bodies and authored replacements against their situations and final decisions.
2. Compared word and character counts for each individual opening. None grew.
3. Compared each complete revised bible with its round-13 source after excluding the intended opening fields. All other content matched exactly, including question payloads, primers, figures, and answer keys.

The master campaign brief now requires this newcomer-readability review, including checking unfamiliar titles and navigation terms. A terminology substitution or a short word count alone does not establish accessibility.
