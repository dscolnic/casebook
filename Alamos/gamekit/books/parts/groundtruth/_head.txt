# --------------------------------------------------------------- glossary
# Every "Worth knowing first" glossary entry in the bible, in the order the
# missions introduce them. `aliases` is the only thing added: it is not copy,
# it is the spellings engine/dev/checkJargon.mjs matches the prose against.
glossary:
# mission 1
- name: Electric charge
  aliases: [electric charge]   # TODO: the plurals and spellings the prose uses
  def: "a property of matter that creates electric force; like signs repel and unlike signs attract. Electric field: force per positive test charge, measured in newtons per coulomb or volts per metre. Superposition: add each source's field as a vector to obtain the net field."
# mission 2
- name: Electric flux
  aliases: [electric flux]   # TODO: the plurals and spellings the prose uses
  def: "the signed amount of electric field passing through a surface. Gaussian surface: an imaginary closed surface chosen to match field symmetry. Surface charge density: charge per area, written σ and measured in C/m²."
# mission 3
- name: Electric potential
  aliases: [electric potential]   # TODO: the plurals and spellings the prose uses
  def: "electric potential energy per charge, measured in volts. Equipotential: a path or surface along which voltage does not change. Line integral: a sum of tiny contributions along a path."
# mission 4
- name: Conductor
  aliases: [conductor]   # TODO: the plurals and spellings the prose uses
  def: "material whose mobile charge rearranges easily. Electrostatic equilibrium: settled state with zero field inside a conductor. Breakdown field: field above which the campaign's air model conducts."
# mission 5
- name: Capacitance
  aliases: [capacitance]   # TODO: the plurals and spellings the prose uses
  def: "stored charge per voltage. Dielectric: insulating material that changes capacitance by polarization. Polarization: small charge separation inside matter."
# mission 6
- name: Marx bank
  aliases: [marx bank]   # TODO: the plurals and spellings the prose uses
  def: "capacitors charged in parallel and discharged in series. Energy density: stored energy per volume. Spark gap: switch that conducts after breakdown."
# mission 7
- name: Current
  aliases: [current]   # TODO: the plurals and spellings the prose uses
  def: "rate of charge flow. Node: connection shared by circuit branches. Reference: voltage point against which a channel is measured."
# mission 8
- name: Magnetic field
  aliases: [magnetic field]   # TODO: the plurals and spellings the prose uses
  def: "field that deflects moving charge and currents. Right-hand rule: hand convention for cross-product direction. Helical motion: circular perpendicular motion plus unchanged parallel motion."
# mission 9
- name: Magnetic flux
  aliases: [magnetic flux]   # TODO: the plurals and spellings the prose uses
  def: "magnetic field passing through a surface. Induced emf: voltage created by changing magnetic flux. Lenz's law: induced current opposes the flux change."
# mission 10
- name: Inductance
  aliases: [inductance]   # TODO: the plurals and spellings the prose uses
  def: "flux linkage per current and opposition to current change. Transient: brief change before a circuit settles. Time constant: characteristic response time."
# mission 11
- name: Current density
  aliases: [current density]   # TODO: the plurals and spellings the prose uses
  def: "current per cross-sectional area. Toroid: ring-shaped winding whose field is mainly inside. Displacement current: changing electric flux term in Ampere-Maxwell law."
# mission 12
- name: Mutual inductance
  aliases: [mutual inductance]   # TODO: the plurals and spellings the prose uses
  def: "flux linkage in one circuit per current in another. Transfer function: output divided by input as a function of frequency."
# mission 13
- name: Bandwidth
  aliases: [bandwidth]   # TODO: the plurals and spellings the prose uses
  def: "range of signal frequencies a channel can follow. RC circuit: resistor-capacitor circuit with τ=RC. RL circuit: resistor-inductor circuit with τ=L/R."
# mission 14
- name: Poynting vector
  aliases: [poynting vector]   # TODO: the plurals and spellings the prose uses
  def: "electromagnetic energy flow per area and direction. Maxwell's equations: four relations linking charge, fields, and changing flux."
