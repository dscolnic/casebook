## The course at grade 6

Middle-school physical science, grade 6: motion, energy and keeping people alive

Roughly follow it; the gaps are useful information. If something important is
taught nowhere, say so in your prose summary.

1. Speed, distance and time, and how they give each other
2. Something moving in space keeps going until something pushes it
3. A push changes motion, and a bigger push changes it faster
4. A small change made early does more than a big one made late
5. Rates: how fast something is used up, and how long it lasts
6. A budget: a finite store shared between competing demands
7. Energy moves from hot to cold, and insulation slows it down
8. Air is a mixture, and the share of each gas has to stay in range
9. Circuits: a break, a short, and why a battery has limits
10. Signals take time to travel, and distance costs strength
11. Spinning things keep spinning, and a nudge takes a while to show
12. Reading a graph and spotting what changed at the moment it changed
13. Repeats and averages, and what one reading is worth
14. Deciding with a deadline that does not move
15. What a measurement can honestly say, and what it cannot
16. Ordering work when several things are wrong at once

## The equations this course may compute

Substitution into a one-line formula, with numbers chosen to stay manageable.
The formula is there to show where the answer comes from, not to be an
arithmetic obstacle.

| Equation | What it is | Taught after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `speed = distance ÷ time` | how fast something is travelling | — |
| `time = distance ÷ speed` | how long a journey or a signal takes | `speed = distance ÷ time` |
| `degrees lost = energy lost ÷ energy for one degree` | how far something cools | — |
| `amount = rate × time` | how much of a store gets used | `speed = distance ÷ time` |
| `how long = amount ÷ rate` | how long a store lasts | — |
| `length of one wave = how far it goes in a second ÷ waves in a second` | how long one radio wave is | — |
| `part = whole × share` | a share of a supply, or of a mixture | — |
| `change in position = change in speed × time` | why an early correction is worth more | `speed = distance ÷ time` |

**Nothing is asked before the equation it is built out of** — `equationOrder`
fails the game for it, and it counts a question that *computes* a number, not
one that mentions the relationship.

### What the senior-high course computes and this one does not

These are in the base game. None of them may be computed here. Where a stop
leans on one, that stop is a redesign, not a rewrite.

- `v = v₀ + at, x = x₀ + v₀t + ½at²` — kinematics at constant acceleration
- `F = ma` — Newton's second law
- `W = Fd, KE = ½mv²` — work, kinetic energy and conservation of energy
- `J = FΔt = Δp` — impulse as the change in momentum
- `τ = rF` — torque about an axis, and rotational equilibrium
- `a_c = v² / r` — centripetal acceleration in circular motion
- `T = 2π√(m/k)` — the period of a simple harmonic oscillator, and resonance
- `P = IV = I²R` — electrical power, and an energy budget over time
- `Q = mcΔT` — heat against a temperature change and a heat capacity
- `v = fλ` — wave speed, frequency and wavelength
