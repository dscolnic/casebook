## The course at grade 6

Middle-school life science, grade 6: traits, inheritance and choosing what to grow

Roughly follow it; the gaps are useful information. If something important is
taught nowhere, say so in your prose summary.

1. Living things pass traits to their offspring
2. A seed is alive, and it can be kept alive or let die
3. Two parents each give half, so the young are not copies
4. A trait can hide in one generation and show up in the next
5. Counting the offspring, and comparing counts with what was expected
6. Punnett squares: writing down what a cross can produce
7. Same seed, different field: what a plant looks like depends on where it grew
8. A fair test needs a control and everything else kept the same
9. Repeats and averages: one plant proves nothing
10. Choosing which plants to breed from, and what that costs
11. Choosing for one trait can drag another along with it
12. Variety inside a collection, and why losing it is hard to undo
13. How many plants you need to keep a kind going
14. A label and a record are part of the seed
15. Disease arrives, and resistance is a trait like any other
16. Deciding with a season that does not come round again

## The equations this course may compute

Substitution into a one-line formula, with numbers chosen to stay manageable.
The formula is there to show where the answer comes from, not to be an
arithmetic obstacle.

| Equation | What it is | Taught after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `part = whole × share` | how many of a batch will do something | — |
| `average = total ÷ how many` | the mean of several plots or plants | — |
| `expected number = how many × the fraction expected` | what a cross should produce | `part = whole × share` |
| `how many times = bigger ÷ smaller` | comparing two yields, or two counts | — |

**Nothing is asked before the equation it is built out of** — `equationOrder`
fails the game for it, and it counts a question that *computes* a number, not
one that mentions the relationship.

### What the senior-high course computes and this one does not

These are in the base game. None of them may be computed here. Where a stop
leans on one, that stop is a redesign, not a rewrite.

- `3 : 1` — the monohybrid ratio, and what a count of a whole generation is evidence for
- `15 : 1` — two genes acting either-way, and the ratio that separates it from one
- `p + q = 1,  p² + 2pq + q² = 1` — Hardy–Weinberg, as the null a collection is measured against
- `recombination frequency ≈ map distance in cM` — linkage: how often a marker and a gene come apart
- `R = h²·S` — the breeder’s equation — response as heritability times the selection differential
- `Nₑ ≈ 4NmNf / (Nm + Nf)` — effective population size, and why a grow-out of thirty is not thirty
