RED SAND - ARCADIA RISE
The places, from themes/redsand/site.js

A propellant plant on Arcadia Planitia. Outdoor site, 17 buildings.
Spawn at (0, 52), yaw 0 - looking straight down the plant line, with the
ascent vehicle on the pad at the end of it.
Terrain 900 m square, player limit 380 m. Far ground opens on sol 5.

Coordinates are (x, z). Looking down, -Z is away from the player at spawn.
Distance is a straight line from the spawn.

THREE THINGS THAT ARE LOAD-BEARING, NOT DECORATIVE

  1. THE GEOGRAPHY IS THE PROCESS. Atmosphere in at the top of the site, water
     out of the ground beside it, hydrogen made in the middle, the two combined
     in the reactor, the product cooled at the far end, and what survives all
     of that stored in the tank farm and loaded into the vehicle. Walking from
     the spawn to the pad is walking one molecule through the whole plant, in
     order - which is why a call at the cold end costs a real part of the sol.

  2. THE ICE CUT'S 338 m IS DELIBERATE. `tiersFor` splits a site at its largest
     distance gap and refuses the split under a ratio of 2, so a place does not
     get a second orientation lap for being fifteen metres further along the
     same track. At z = -232 the cut sat 300 m out against the pad office at
     163: ratio 1.84, and the site stayed one tier. 338 / 163 = 2.07 is what
     earns the far tier. The far ground then opens on sol 5 rather than the
     default 4, so the ice cut does not share its opening with the intake.

  3. TWO PLACEMENTS HAD TO MOVE, AND NO CHECK COULD SEE EITHER.
     - The Pad Office first went to (-26, -120), whose door at (-26, -112) the
       walk check could not reach from the spawn: the berm is a 30 m ring at
       (0, -132) and the only approach ran through it. The berm is what makes
       the pad read as a pad, so the office moved to (-46, -104) instead.
     - The Tank Farm and its three cryogenic tanks first sat astride the track.
       A straight-line route probe walked into them. It is east of the track now.
     Also: the spawn at (0, 52) has nothing within twelve metres. A prop over
     the spawn welds the player in place - the scene renders and W does nothing.
