Where these come from

Every line above is read straight out of the ratchet files in gamekit/engine/dev/, against the shipped content of carrying on 1 September 2026, branch deep-watch-integration. Confirm the zero for yourself:

cd gamekit
npm run check carrying       # every gate, this campaign only
node engine/dev/checkStory.mjs carrying    # the banked lines, printed as notes

Carrying Capacity came in with the first revised bundle (Alamos/revised_games_bundle/, committed in 8eeaf5c), which is why it is quiet: its debt was banked when it landed. The nine campaigns of the second bundle (all_revised_games_latest/) were landed under an instruction to overrule content gates, so their debt is failing rather than banked — 66 live failures against this campaign’s nought.

Paying any of it down

  • Fix the cards, then re-bank: node engine/dev/checkStory.mjs carrying --write-debt rewrites the list files. The two number files fall the same way.
  • Read alamos-accessibility before touching a reading level — the seven defects to find in a card first, and the rule that the official term stays and gets glossed. A grade bought by deleting vocabulary is what spw is gated against.
  • Do not delete a debt line to silence a gate. A line whose card now passes fails on its own, which is the mechanism working, not a false alarm.
