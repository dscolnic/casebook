# What is on the walls at Ridgeway

Not teaching material. None of this is on the syllabus, none of it is checked, and
a player who reads all of it learns no physics. It is here because a laboratory
where every notice is a safety instruction is a laboratory nobody works in, and the
things that make a place look lived in are the things nobody would ever write down:
the rota, the leaving card, the note about the washing up.

Written per room, because a notice belongs somewhere. `wordedSign` in
`engine/world/interiorKit.js` renders any of these — `{ tag, heading, body, footer,
accent }` — and murals are a painted panel rather than a sheet: bigger, no frame,
lower contrast.

House rules for this material:

- **Undated where possible.** "Back Tuesday" ages better than a date that is
  always wrong.
- **No jokes that need a second read.** A player walks past at 1.4 m/s.
- **Nothing that contradicts the story.** No notice may announce a result the
  campaign has not reached, and none may name a person who is not on the roster.
- **Curling, not crisp.** Two or three should be visibly older than the rest —
  a yellowed edge is worth more than another sheet.

---

## The corridor

The longest wall in the building and the one everybody walks. Boards here are
building-wide.

| Kind | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Notice board | **THIS WEEK** — who is on shift, who is away, who has the fridge. Half the slots filled in pencil, one crossed out twice. |
| Notice board | **SEMINARS** — a term's worth of talks, three of them in the past, one with "MOVED — see email" written across it. |
| Poster | Last year's group photograph outside the building, everybody squinting. |
| Poster | A conference poster somebody presented and never took down, corner curling. |
| Sheet | **LOST PROPERTY** — one glove, a bike light, a mug with a duck on it. Ask at sign-in. |
| Sheet | **COFFEE FUND** — honesty box. "£1 a cup, £2 if you take the last one and don't make more." |
| Sheet | **BIKE STORE IS FULL** — do not chain to the handrail, the fire officer has noticed. |
| Small frame | The building's original opening plaque, and a photograph of the site as a car park. |
| Mural | A long, low band of colour keyed to the temperature gradient — warm at the south end, cold at the north. Unlabelled. Nobody in the story admits it is deliberate. |
| Whiteboard | A half-erased seating plan for somebody's leaving lunch. |

## Sign-in & Interlocks

The first wall anybody sees, and the one with the most rules on it.

- **VISITORS** — sign in, badge on, escorted at all times past the yellow line.
- **WHAT MAY NOT COME IN** — a pictogram list: no watches, no keys, no loose tools
  past the shielded room, no food beyond the office.
- A visitor badge board with three badges missing and a note: *"Whoever has 07,
  it is not yours."*
- **FIRE** — assembly point, wardens, and the date of the last drill.
- A framed photograph of the first chip the group ever cooled, mounted crookedly.
- A child's drawing, taped up: a fridge with a smiling face on it.

## Group Office

Where the argument about publishing happens, so the walls are about people.

- A wall planner, the fortnight to the review blocked out in red.
- **LEAVING CARD** — pinned open for signatures, a pen on a string beside it.
- Postcards from six conferences, overlapping, one upside down.
- A printed list: **WHO KNOWS HOW TO FIX THE PRINTER** (three names, two crossed out).
- A takeaway menu with a phone number circled hard enough to tear the paper.
- Somebody's marathon number, kept.
- A photograph of the group at a summer barbecue, mostly out of focus.

## Fabrication & Materials

Yellow light, gowning, and the discipline that goes with it.

- **GOWNING ORDER** — the pictogram sequence, laminated, thumbed at one corner.
- **THIS BAY IS BOOKED** — a whiteboard grid, the week, initials in most squares.
- A wafer map printed at A3, defects circled in green, taped at three corners.
- **NO PAPER PAST THIS LINE** — and, underneath, in different handwriting,
  *"this includes the crossword."*
- A run-log clipboard hanging on a nail.
- A poster of the fabrication process as a flow chart, faded, one step tippexed out
  and rewritten.

## Cryogenics & Vacuum

Cold, heavy, and the room with the most hazard on the wall.

- **OXYGEN DEFICIENCY** — the standard warning, with the monitor's minimum written
  in marker underneath.
- **FRIDGE TIME** — the booking sheet, four days a slot, three months out, entirely
  full.
- A hand-drawn cooldown curve from a good run, kept up because it was a good run.
- **DEWAR RETURNS** — collection days, and *"label it or it comes back to you."*
- A photograph of the fridge with its shields off, from the year it was installed.
- A tally chart on the door frame: cooldowns this year, in fives.

## Control & Readout

Desks, screens, and shift discipline.

- **HANDOVER** — a printed pro forma, half of them filled in and clipped to the wall.
- A rack elevation drawing, annotated in three colours by three people.
- **DO NOT SWITCH OFF** — taped to a specific socket, no explanation given.
- A photograph of the first Rabi oscillation the group measured, printed and framed.
- A dartboard, low, with a strict "not during a run" note beside it.
- Somebody's daughter's drawing of a control desk with far too many buttons.

## Microwave Racks

A cupboard of equipment, so the walls are inventory.

- **CABLE LOAN** — a sign-out sheet, six lines used, two returned.
- A rack layout diagram with two units drawn in pencil that were never installed.
- **BROKEN — DO NOT USE** tags, three of them, hanging on a hook rather than on the
  equipment.
- A torque table for SMA connectors, greasy at one corner.

## Error & Verification

The room that argues about numbers.

- **RESULTS BOARD** — printouts of the same measurement from three sessions, pinned
  side by side, differences ringed.
- A whiteboard nobody has cleaned, with a plot sketched and *"ASK ANDERS"* under it.
- **REPORTING** — a short list of what travels with a number when it leaves the room.
- A photograph of a whiteboard, printed and pinned to the whiteboard.

## Analysis Desks

Open plan, so the walls are personal.

- Four desk calendars, none on the right month.
- A printed plot with a hand-drawn arrow and the word *"why?"*.
- **HEADPHONES ON = DO NOT ASK ME THINGS** — printed in a jokey font, entirely serious.
- A postcard of a cat sitting in a cardboard box, no caption, universally understood.
- A wall clock two minutes fast, and a note explaining that it is deliberate.

## Networks & Security

The room where paperwork is the point.

- **NOTHING LEAVES ON REMOVABLE MEDIA** — with a printed refusal form beneath it.
- A fibre route diagram of the campus, one span highlighted and dated.
- **KEYS** — a cabinet with a signing-out sheet and a pencil on a chain.
- A photograph of the first link lit, two people shaking hands in a plant room.

## Quantum Sensing

The room that talks to people outside the building.

- **CALIBRATION DUE** — a wall chart, monthly, with the hospital study's name on it.
- A thank-you card from the hospital department, pinned where visitors see it.
- A framed press cutting about the study, small, from a local paper.
- **QUIET PLEASE — MEASURING** — a sign on a hook, hung out when it matters.
- A photograph of the magnetometer being carried in through a door that was too small.

## Reading Room

The one room with no equipment in it.

- A noticeboard of thesis defence announcements, the oldest three years old.
- **PLEASE RESHELVE** — and a shelf that plainly nobody has.
- A print of an old star chart, chosen because it is beautiful and for no other reason.
- A jigsaw half done on a side table, with a note asking people to leave it.
- A mural: a plain, quiet wash of colour across one wall, no diagram, no labels.

## Gas & Cryogen Store

- **CYLINDER STOCK** — a chalked tally, full and empty, updated daily.
- **CHAIN EVERY CYLINDER** — with a photograph of one that was not.
- Delivery days, and the gate code, half rubbed out.
- A first-aid poster for cold burns, laminated, unread.

## Shielded Room

The last door, and the strictest.

- **CLOSE THE DOOR BEHIND YOU** — large, and the only notice on that wall.
- A checklist of what must be left outside, in six lines.
- Inside: nothing at all on the walls. The emptiness is the point, and it is the one
  room in the building where a bare wall is right.
