SAFETY FACTOR (theme: midway) — PLACES AND LOCATIONS
Corbin Park, a closed lakeside amusement park, three weeks before it tries to
reopen. AP Physics 1, all eight units, taught through derivations. Grade 11.

Sources: themes/midway/site.js, fixtures.js, minors.js, interiors.js,
content/groups.js, props.js. Coordinates are (x, z); -Z is away from the player
at the gate. Distance is measured from the spawn at (0, 58).

Run it:  cd gamekit && THEME=midway npm run dev

THE RULE THAT SHAPES THIS LIST
  The rides ARE the areas. Every area is a ride, so every group is a station or
  plant room at the foot of a machine — and the machines, which are the whole
  silhouette of the place, are props in props.js standing over them. A player
  navigates by machines, not by buildings.

  This park cannot earn a far tier and never will. tiersFor wants a distance
  ratio of 2.0 and at least 120 m; the largest gap in the list below is 27 m and
  the best ratio is 1.32. A midway is walked end to end in ninety seconds. So
  what grows here is DOORS, not ground — three modelled buildings open on the day
  the campaign first sends somebody to them. Ground is never fenced; the whole
  park is walkable from the first morning.

WHEEL — The Ferris Wheel                        (-18, 22)   40 m from spawn
  Building:  Wheel Machine Room, 16 x 11 x 4.6, facing 0
  Machine:   a 28 m wheel — 24 arms, 24 gondolas hanging level from their pins,
             two A-frames and the axle they carry. A crane is parked where arm
             nine comes down.
  Teaches:   torque and rotation. Balance the wheel on paper; the load the arm
             actually sees; a moment into a stress; a wind limit somebody can
             operate to.
  Fixtures (at: targets):
    hub-schedule    board, back  — 24 arms, 8 bolts a hub, one drawing from 1945
    gondola-shell   bench, left  — down for painting; a flat side, wind gets all of it
    brake-drum      vessel,right — a band on a drum, 81 years of glaze on the lining
    arm-nine-file   board, back  — from day 8; a 41 mm indication, sized, not judged

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SHIP — The Pirate Ship                          (42, 6)     67 m from spawn
  Building:  Pirate Ship Console, 14 x 10 x 4.2, facing -PI/2
  Machine:   the swinging ship on its A-frame.
  Teaches:   simple harmonic motion. Measure the period and get g out of it;
             derive why the period ignores the load; the drive frequency the ride
             must avoid; certify the swing angle.
  Fixtures:
    arm-trestles    bench, left  — 8.6 m of swing arm off the ride, pivot to the door
    drive-console   board, back  — Idowu's console: drive interval, seat count
    seat-frame      rack,  right — one seat of sixty, at the height it hangs at
    timing-trace    board, back  — from day 8; 6.15 s of swing vs 5.85 s of drive

CAROUSEL — The Carousel and Swings               (-44, -4)   76 m from spawn
  Building:  Carousel Drive House, 14 x 10 x 4.2, facing PI/2
  Machine:   carousel with cones round it; the chair swings beside it.
  Teaches:   circular motion. Separate speed from acceleration; derive the angle

    controller-crate   rack,  back  — UNTIL day 6; the new controller, still crated

COASTER — The Coaster                            (-46, -44)  112 m from spawn
  Building:  Coaster Station, 26 x 12 x 5.0, facing PI/2 (east, onto the midway —
             facing it north put its door under the track with the standing train
             across the approach, which only the reachability fill caught)
  Machine:   the one prop in the repo that could not be built out of the kit — a
             tube swept along a spline: station, 26 m chain lift, first drop,
             valley, a vertical loop of radius 7.4 with its crown at 20 m, a long
             banked turn and the brake run back in. A bent to the ground every
             six metres. The train stands where it stopped in October.
  Teaches:   energy. Close the energy books on one circuit; find where the losses
             actually are; derive the speed the loop demands; recompute the
             margin the drawing promised.
  Fixtures:
    profile-drawing  board, back  — the 1974 track profile, drawn once, never redrawn
    station-wheel    bench, left  — Kovač's speed wheel, same bracket for 3 springs
    lift-panel       board, right — chain motor amps, rating plate underneath
    crown-tape       board, back  — from day 10; 7.4 m pinned beside a drawing saying 5.6

  Machine:   45 m lattice tower; copper fin strip up one face with the magnet

  Teaches:   kinematics. Time the drop and get an acceleration out of it; derive

================================================================================
2. THE THREE ENTERABLE BUILDINGS — doors, not ground
================================================================================
Not areas. `enter:` gives each an interiors key without making it a group; each
holds one sited call, and access.js keeps the door sealed and unnamed until the
day the campaign sends somebody through it. A stop whose `at:` resolves under one
of these keys is asked THERE, while the question still belongs to its own area.

WORKSHOP — "Brennan's bench, and eleven notebooks"   (-54, 46)   opens day 5
  20 x 13 x 5.4, facing PI/2. Sub: the eleven notebooks the whole campaign is
  about. The drop test is a written procedure before it is a drop, and it is
  written here because the order it has to run in is the order Brennan never
  wrote down.
    bench-notebooks   bench, back — 41 years of settings in one hand, in date
                                    order for the first time

PLANT ROOM — "Everything on the midway runs off this board"   (-60, -70)  day 10
  14 x 10 x 4.4, facing PI/2. The pump's duty is a lift and a flow; what says
  whether the park may ask for it is the motor plate and the breaker feeding it,
  and both are here rather than in the pumphouse.
    motor-plate       board, left — 55 kW on the plate

ARCADE AND STALLS — "Boarded, and not on the list"   (40, 44)   opens day 11
  24 x 12 x 5.0, facing PI. The one thing in the park nobody has to certify,
  which is why it is the only place the physics can be played with.
    stall-cannon      bench, back — a nozzle on a pivot, three marks on the back
                                    wall, a pressure gauge

3. PLACES THAT CARRY THE PARK, NOT A LESSON
================================================================================
FRONT GATE           (0, 74)   24 x 9 x 4.6, "Closed since October". Stays a
                     facade on purpose — it is shut because the park is shut, and
                     it opens when the certificates are signed, which is after
                     the last day the player sees. A room behind it would be a
                     room whose whole point is that you cannot get in yet.
REOPENING BOARD      (11, 60)  facing PI. The delivery board: The Corbin Park
                     Certificate, a piece a day, seven rides waiting on it.
SPAWN                (0, 58)   yaw 0 — looks up the midway, wheel on the left,
                     ship on the right, coaster's lift hill behind them. Clear
                     for twelve metres, and it must stay clear.
THE MIDWAY           one avenue from the gate to the lake (cx 0, w 14, d 180),
                     with cross paths at z = 22 and z = -52. Ground furniture is
                     kept off it (|x| < 8) so nothing narrows the walk.
SERVICE ROAD         (-62, -20), w 8, d 150 — round the back of the rides.
TWO CAR PARKS        (-56, 108) and (56, 108), 96 x 64 each, with the approach
                     apron at (0, 92) and the county route across z = 146. Eight
                     hundred cars, a hundred and six selling days a year.
THE LAKE             cx 0, cz -190, 460 x 150, level -1.4. The flume's water.
                     Open water to the north; wooded hills across the south
                     behind the car park. That asymmetry is the wayfinding —
                     trees on the skyline means you are facing away from the lake.
PLAYER LIMIT         165 m (the car parks reach z = 140).

================================================================================
4. AUTHORING NOTES
================================================================================
· A lesson points at a fixture with `at: <id>` in books/midway.yml. A lesson that
  points at nothing is answered at the case stand — which is every
  decision-format call in this book, deliberately: a decision is made with a
  person, not at a bench.
· Fixtures are declared by name and wall, never by coordinate; interiorFixtures.js
  computes position from room.bounds. build: vessel | rack | bench | board.
  wall: left | right | back. along: -1 … 1, 0 is the middle.
· `from:` / `until:` gate a fixture by day — the taped crown radius, the witnessed
  drop test, the operator's card, the arm nine file, the crated controller.
· Every `group` in site.js must be a group id in content/groups.js, one per ride.
  A group with no building is a call the player cannot reach; worldParity and
  reachable are what notice.
· The lights come back on as the campaign does: a state hook runs the festoon
  lighting over the midway off the number of rides certified.
· Never place a prop over the spawn or across the avenue. A prop over the spawn
  welds the player in place — the scene renders and W does nothing.
