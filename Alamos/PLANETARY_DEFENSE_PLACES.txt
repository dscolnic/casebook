PLANETARY DEFENSE (theme: planetary_defense) — PLACES AND LOCATIONS
The Cerro Alto range, at night. Astronomy — discovery, astrometry,
characterisation, radar, impact physics. Grade 12. dayNoun: 'Phase'.
Campaign Director · International NEO Response.

Sources: themes/planetary_defense/site.js, fixtures.js, minors.js, props.js,
theme.js, content/groups.js. Coordinates are (x, z) in metres; distance is
measured from the spawn at (2, 62), on the road below the coordination office.

Run it:  cd gamekit && THEME=planetary_defense npm run dev

THE RULE THAT SHAPES THIS LIST
  This is a RANGE, not a campus — the one game in the set whose sites are
  kilometres apart, and the one played in the dark. Both facts come from the
  same place: an observatory works nights, and its instruments cannot live
  together. A megawatt planetary radar beside an optical telescope ruins both.
  A survey dome wants the darkest, highest ground there is. A consequences lab
  wants to be near the emergency managers it briefs, which means down in the
  valley with the road and the phones.

  Base camp is walkable and holds two of the six areas, so a phase that stays
  there is a phase on foot. Everything else is driven — or, from phase 4 when
  the aircraft is signed out, flown. Every site has a lit pad and a beacon: a
  landing site you cannot see from the air, at night, is a landing site you fly
  past.

  The range was twice this size and the sites read as unreachable rather than
  far — nine minutes of flying to a dome with nothing to look at on the way.
  Every distance from base camp is now half what it was, with summit radii and
  heights halved too, so the slopes are the same grade.

FOUR LANDFORMS, 2.5 km ACROSS   (terrain size 2800, playerLimit 1300, basin -96)

2. THE ONE ENTERABLE NON-AREA
================================================================================
TOWN — Valle Seco Emergency Office         "The people the numbers are about"
  (95, 810)   754 m   16 x 11 x 4.6, facing PI. Forty-six metres from the Entry
  & Consequences Lab — the same corner of the range.
  Stood modelled, lit and shut from the day the theme shipped: an area is what
  gets an interior in this engine, and Valle Seco carries no group. `enter:
  'TOWN'` gives it an interiors key without making it an area — a door, a room,
  no case stand and no delivery board. Three day-14 stops are asked here
  (matching action to evidence, the displaced-population estimate, and writing
  the evacuation thresholds); they still belong to OPS, and the player is sent
  to Valle Seco to answer them because that is where the office is.
  Fixtures:
    evac-desk        bench, left — two counties' worth of people, and a plan that
                                   costs trust nobody gets back
    threshold-board  board, back — what happens at each stage, written down
                                   before the next update can panic anybody into
                                   changing it
  Panel (minors.js): population under the corridor 9,000,000 (alarm) ·
    evacuation lead time needed ~30 days (high) · plans funded 0 of 2 counties
    (low) · warning time at discovery 11 days (alarm).
    Stand line: "Stockpiles and plans are cheap to undo. Moving nine million
    people is neither."

================================================================================
4. AUTHORING AND WORLD NOTES
================================================================================
· A lesson points at a fixture with `at: <id>` in books/planetary-defense.yml.
  A lesson pointing at nothing is answered at the case stand.
· Fixtures are declared by name and wall, never by coordinate.
  build: vessel | rack | bench | board. wall: left | right | back.
  along: -1 … 1, 0 is the middle.
· travelSpeed: 16 — the phase budget is measured at driving speed, not the
  helicopter's cruise of 34. The first three phases are driven, and a budget
  written for the aircraft leaves a driven phase with no time to think in.
· aircraftFromDay: 4 — before that the machine is on the pad and says so, and
  the range is crossed in the site truck.
· mapRadius: 170 — the map draws a window around the player, not the whole
  range. Drawn whole, base camp (seven buildings inside 200 m) came out a
  centimetre across and unreadable. Anything outside the window is an arrow on
  the edge with its distance.
· NIGHT: written as a nocturnal game and currently overridden to daylight —
  `look.dayWindow: [8, 20]`, with the night sky, fog and light rig still tuned
  for [19, 31] and one line away from coming back. `nightSky: 0x05070d` is used
  only below deep night; it is left in place so restoring the window restores
  the night sky with it.
· No real lights anywhere in props.js. Everything that glows is an emissive
  material — point lights are how a floor went from 118 fps to 20.
· The shutter is the whole silhouette. A closed dome is a grain silo; an open
  one with a slot of darker sky and a tube pointing out of it is unmistakably a
  telescope at two hundred metres in the dark.
· Wayfinding: high country stands north and west; the south-east falls away to
  the valley the road climbs from. That is the one bearing where the sky comes
  down to the horizon — and the direction the survey telescope spends its night
  pointing.
· Scrub only low down (band 350–1150 m from origin); the summits are bare rock.
· Every `group` in site.js must exist in content/groups.js or that area's calls
  are unreachable. Never place a prop over the spawn.
