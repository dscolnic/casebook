Verdict

The best junior edition in the repo, on every number I can measure. per-day is [3,3,3,3,3,3,3,3,3,3]. Nine — Review variants, so six of ten days carry a callback and a child meets each idea twice. Zero rows in every debt file. 5 of 5 syllabus relations computed, 15 of 15 concepts touched, and zero select-only concepts — the only campaign in the entire catalogue, senior or junior, with all three of those at once. Zero numeral damage. questionLoad reports 6 of 39 demanding stops (15%), and every one of the six is a board-size note rather than a reading problem. Fourteen distinct formats across thirty stops, including a CLOUD, a TRACE, a STRESS and an ATTEST at grade 6.

And the content is not softened. The six boards are the parent's arithmetic at a sixth grader's pace — the one-in-six pick rate, what an eye can make out at twenty-two metres, the illuminance on the doorway, the functional size of the array from mock witnesses, a centimetre of face at the surveyed distance, and what nineteen hours does to a score. The parent's whole method survives: measure the distance instead of arguing about it, date every claim in the file, and show the photographs to people who were never there rather than asking a witness to be sure one more time.

The ending is the bravest in the junior set. It names two people the report damaged — Alma Cardoza, who read in a newspaper that the identification she gave in good faith had been taken apart, and Karen Loomis, named in a judgment for running a line-up the way the rules told her to — and it says the court "said plainly that they were not finding him innocent." At grade 6.

Answerable: 30/30. Sense: Excellent, and cumulative in the same way the parent is. Level: Right, and written against the gate from the first stop rather than swept afterwards. Fun: High in a register nothing else in the junior set uses — forensic rather than dramatic.

  • Written to the gate. Zero debt rows, 100% of equations computed, no select-only concepts, no numeral damage. None of that is luck.
  • Nine review variants. Six of ten days carry a callback. Thirty-seven campaigns have none.
  • It kept the hard ideas and shortened the sentences, which is the whole distinction MIDDLE_SCHOOL_EDITIONS.md insists on and that nine of the first-pass editions got wrong.
