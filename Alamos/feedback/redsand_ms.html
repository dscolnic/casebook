Red Sand (grade 6 edition) — play-through review

Theme redsand_ms · middle-school physical science, grade 6 · 10 days, 30 stops · reviewed 2026-08-21 by reading books/redsand-ms.yml in full, working every board, and comparing against the parent redsand.

Verdict

One of the two best junior editions in the repo, and the reason is structural: redsand_ms was written against questionLoad from the first stop rather than swept afterwards, and it shows in every number. per-day is [3,3,3,3,3,3,3,3,3,3] — thirty stops, ten days, no exceptions, the only junior edition besides the_trial_ms and sightline_ms with that discipline. It carries no rows in any debt file. It computes 5 of 5 syllabus relations. questionLoad reports 3 of 30 demanding stops (8%). And it is one of only two junior editions with nine — Review variants, so it is one of five campaigns in the entire catalogue where a player actually meets an idea a second time — eight of its ten days carry a callback.

The nine boards are correct and the register is right: 340 L ÷ 85 L/min = 4.0 minutes in the bed; 13.8 × 44.0 ÷ 16.0 = 37.9 kg of carbon dioxide a sol; ΔT_f = 3 × 1.86 × 8.0 ≈ 45 K below pure water's freezing point. The free-energy, reaction-quotient, Faraday and dust-attenuation boards are all present in simplified form.

The parent's plot survives whole, which is the thing worth recording: the window opens on sol 486 and not again for twenty-six months; eleven kilograms a sol against a schedule needing fourteen; Sundqvist wants the reactor hotter and Herrera says a hotter bed hands back less; and the actual fault is a poisoned catalyst nobody was looking at. Both sides of the argument are named on the opening card with their positions stated, which eleven campaigns in this catalogue do not manage.

Answerable: 30/30. Sense: Excellent, and the ending is the best in the junior set — see below. Level: Right, and written to the gate rather than at it. Fun: High. "Six people go home on whatever those two can be made to agree."

What the derivation did well, and what the set should copy

  • Three authored stops a day, ten days, and nine review variants. That combination is what makes the day model's spaced retrieval fire. Only redsand_ms, sightline_ms, redsand, sightline and hospital have it, out of forty-two campaigns.
  • Written to the gate, not swept afterwards. MIDDLE_SCHOOL_EDITIONS.md §9 records this as a different and cheaper process, and the numbers bear it out: 8% demanding stops, no debt rows, 100% of equations computed.
  • Both sides named on the opening card, with positions. "Ingrid Sundqvist, who runs production, wants the reactor run hotter tonight. Tomas Herrera, who owns the reactor, says a hotter bed hands back less every pass."
  • The ending is the best in the junior set: "the last four hundred kilograms came from a catalyst charge nobody wanted to fit, running on water nobody had been cleaning properly, and both of those were found because somebody kept asking where the missing kilograms had gone." Then the unfinished item is a rule written on paper with no alarm behind it, "and the next crew will find out whether a rule is enough."
